Register lab Advanced

Hunt for a hidden switch in the watch's display-settings register (13), for example a day-first date order. The scan backs the register up, flips each of its bits once, keeps note of which ones the watch stores, and puts every bit back.

  1. Connect the watch and keep it in front of you. Press no buttons except (B).
  2. Tap Scan for hidden bits (about a minute). The display may flicker as bits change.
  3. For each byte the watch keeps, step through its values and watch the date line.
  4. Tap Restore backup when you're done, unless you found a value you want to keep.

If the watch drops the connection, hold (C) for 3 seconds, reconnect and tap Restore backup. If it won't reconnect, turn off the watch's Airplane Mode, or reset its settings as the manual describes.
